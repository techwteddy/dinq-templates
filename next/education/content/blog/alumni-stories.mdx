## Innovation in Silicon Valley

**Marcus Zhang (Class of '22)**
_Current Role: Senior AI Researcher at a leading tech firm_

Marcus was one of the first students to dive into our advanced AI curriculum.

> "The hands-on labs and the mentorship from Dr. Rodriguez were pivotal," Marcus recalls. "I wasn't just learning theory; I was building models that solved real problems. That mindset of practical application is what sets our graduates apart."

## Ethical Leadership in Finance

**Elena Moretti (Class of '20)**
_Current Role: Director of Sustainable Investment at a global bank_

Elena credits her success to the interdisciplinary approach of the Business department. "My minor in Environmental Science, combined with a strong grounding in business ethics, allowed me to see opportunities where others saw conflicts. Our institution taught me that profit and purpose aren't mutually exclusive."

## Creativity in the Digital Age

**Jordan Lee (Class of '23)**
_Current Role: Creative Lead at a major digital media house_

Jordan's path was shaped by the convergence of art and technology. "The Digital Arts program gave me the tools to express my vision in ways I never thought possible. But more importantly, it gave me a community of collaborators who continue to inspire me today."

## Advice for Current Students

When asked what advice they would give to those currently walking the halls (or navigating the virtual classrooms), our alumni were unanimous:

1.  **Be Curious:** Don't limit yourself to your major. The most valuable skills often lie at the intersection of disciplines.
2.  **Build Your Network:** The connections you make here will last a lifetime.
3.  **Seek Mentorship:** Don't be afraid to ask for guidance. Our faculty are here to help you succeed.
