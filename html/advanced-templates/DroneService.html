03  ·  How It Works

From Takeoff
to Delivered

A streamlined process designed to make aerial filming effortless — from the first call to final delivery.

✈️ Ready to fly?
Most projects launch within 3 days of booking.
01
Consultation & Site Scout
We start with a call to understand your property, goals, and timeline. Our team then scouts the location — checking airspace clearances, FAA authorizations, optimal flight paths, and the ideal time of day for golden-hour light.
02
Aerial Capture Day
Our pilot arrives fully equipped with professional-grade DJI Inspire 3 and Mavic 3 Pro equipment. We capture everything — sweeping establishing shots, precise property angles, neighborhood context, and close-up detail shots in 4K HDR.
03
Professional Edit & Color Grade
Our editors apply cinematic color grading, stabilization, and licensed music scoring. Every edit is crafted specifically for your property's unique character and target buyer — never templated, never generic.
04
Delivery Within 48 Hours
You receive a private link to your full 4K video, edited highlight reel, still photography gallery, and ready-to-embed virtual tour. All files formatted for MLS, social media, and your website — ready to post immediately.
